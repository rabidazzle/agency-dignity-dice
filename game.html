    How to Play

    You take care of a snack machine. There is no money and no timer. Take all the time you like.

    1. Look at the task list to see what the machine needs.
    2. Tap a snack or a tool in your supply cart to pick it up.
    3. Tap the slot on the machine where it belongs. That’s it!
    4. Fill every slot, fix any problems, and clean the glass to finish the shift.

    What the icons mean:

    🔧Wrench — fixes a jam (a snack is stuck).
    🧽Cloth — wipes a smudge off the glass.
    🗑️Bin — removes an old snack that is past its date.

    If something doesn’t fit, the machine gives you a friendly hint. Nothing bad happens — just try again.
