Which of the Unburied are you?

Five ancient evils. Five prisons. One cauldron that frees them. Answer eight questions about hunger, control, and the long quiet of a cell that does not end. Find out which of the five you would have been.

Morenth pulled them from the wound he had been widening in reality for centuries. A Qeth who outgrew its kin. A half-dragon, half-minotaur in stone-frozen rage. The First Orc, mid-charge, one eye blazing. A cat who outlived every bridle. A void that wears your face.

They drank from the cauldron. They were given knowledge of every age that had passed without them. Then they were sent into the world.
